To understand financial accounting deeply, we must first establish what constitutes a "Theory". Major authoritative definitions include:

  • The Oxford English Dictionary: A scheme or system of ideas or statements held as an explanation of a group of facts or phenomena.
  • Hendriksen (1970): A coherent set of hypothetical, conceptual and pragmatic principles forming the general framework of reference for a field of inquiry.
  • FASB (US Financial Accounting Standards Board): A coherent system of interrelated objectives and fundamentals that can lead to consistent standards.

The core of these definitions relies on the word "coherent", meaning components must logically combine without contradiction. Theories are not "ad hoc"; they are built on systematic and logical reasoning rather than single, isolated purposes.

يعني إيه "نظرية" في المحاسبة؟ النظرية دي مش كلام دش للحفظ، دي "الدماغ والنظام" اللي ورا كل قيد ومعيار بنعمله في المكاتب والشركات. المنهج حاطط لك ٣ تعريفات أساسية لازم تفهم الكلمة المميزة في كل واحد منهم عشان الـ MCQ والـ المقالي:

  • ١. قاموس أكسفورد (Oxford): بيقولك النظرية دي عبارة عن سيستم من الأفكار معمول مخصوص عشان يفسر (Explanation) ظواهر أو حقائق بنشوفها في السوق.
  • ٢. العالم هندريكسن (Hendriksen): ده ركز على إنها برنامج متكامل من المبادئ العلمية والعملية اللي بتعمل لنا إطار مرجعي عام (General framework of reference) نرجعله لما نيجي نبحث في المحاسبة.
  • ٣. مجلس المعايير الأمريكي (FASB): وده تعريفه يعتبر "معياري" وبيقول إنها سيستم بيربط الأهداف بالأساسيات عشان نوصل في الآخر لـ معايير محاسبية ثابتة وموحدة (Consistent standards).
🚨 تكة امتحان خطيرة :
• كلمة Coherent (متماسك) معناها إن أجزاء النظرية لازم تركب على بعضها بالمنطق وميبقاش فيها أي تناقض.
• النظرية عمرها ما تكون Not Ad hoc (ليست عشوائية أو مؤقتة)، يعني مش بنألف فكرة سريعة عشان نحل مشكلة ليلة امبارح ونرميها الصبح، لأ.. النظرية مبنية على تفكير منطقي منظم ومستمر.

كثير من الطلاب بيقولوا "أنا مالي ومال النظرية ما أنا هطلع اشتغل بالبرامج والقيود!"، الكتاب هنا بيرد عليك بقوة وبيقولك إنك لو عايز تفضل مجرد مدخل بيانات (Data Entry) متدرسش نظرية، لكن لو عايز تبقى مستشار مالي أو مدير حسابات شاطر (CFO) لازم تدرسها للأسباب دي:

  • ١. التحليل المالي الذكي (Financial Analysis): لما تطلع سوق العمل وتيجي تحلل القوائم المالية عشان تاخد قرار استثماري أو تمويلي، مش هتعرف تقرأ اللي بين السطور إلا لو فاهم النظرية اللي الأرقام دي اتبنت عليها.

  • ٣. التقييم والنقد (Evaluation): النظرية بتديك "برواز ومنظومة علمية" تخليك قادر تحكم على المعايير الجديدة وتعرف هل هي بتعكس واقع الشركة صح ولا محتاجة تطوير.

Accounting research and theories are strictly classified into two main paradigms:

1. Positive Accounting Theories (PAT):
  • Objective: Seeks to predict and explain particular accounting phenomena and actual practices.
  • Focus: Explains *why* managers choose specific accounting methods (e.g., electing one depreciation method over another when confronted with a choice).
  • Key Examples: Positive Accounting Theory (PAT) by Watts and Zimmerman, alongside Legitimacy theory, Institutional theory, and Stakeholder theory (Detailed in Chapter 8).
2. Normative Accounting Theories:
  • Objective: Seeks to prescribe what particular practice should be undertaken, regardless of current actual practices.
  • Focus: Generated by applying a specific norm, standard, or objective against reality. It may suggest radical changes.
  • Key Examples: Alternatives to Historical Cost during inflation (e.g., Present Value, Current Purchasing Power CPP, Current Cost Accounting CCA, and Fair Value) (Detailed in Chapter 5).

🟢 أولاً: النظرية الإيجابية (Positive Theory) — "بتاعة أرض الواقع":
دي مش بتعدل على حد، دي بتنزل السوق تشوف المحاسبين والمديرين بيعملوا إيه، وتحاول تفسره وتتوقعه (Predict & Explain). بتدرس مصلحة المدير وسلوكه؛ يعني لو المدير قدامه طريقتين لتقييم المخزون، النظرية دي بتدرس وتتوقع هو هيختار أنهي طريقة وليه (غالباً عشان يزود أرباحه فياخد مكافأة أكبر!).

🔵 ثانياً: النظرية المعيارية (Normative Theory) — "بتاعة المثالية واللي المفروض يكون":
دي بقى بتقولك الواقع ده فيه عيوب ولازم نصلحه! بتهتم بكلمة Prescribe (توصف أو تفرض) وكلمة Should be (ما يجب أن يكون). الباحث هنا بيحط هدف أو معيار مثالي ويقيس عليه الواقع.
• أمثلة المنهج: وقت التضخم والغلاء، التكلفة التاريخية بتطلع أرقام مضللة. يجي الباحث المعياري يقولك: "إحنا المفروض نلغي التكلفة التاريخية ونقيس بـ القيمة الحالية (Present Value) أو القيمة العادلة".

📌 كبسولة البصمجة السريعة:
• Positive: Explains and predicts WHAT IS (بيفسر الواقع الحالي).
• Normative: Prescribes WHAT SHOULD BE (بيفرض اللي المفروض يحصل).

In this chapter we consider various prescriptive theories of accounting (normative theories) that were advanced on the basis that historical cost accounting has too many shortcomings, particularly in times of rising prices and changing market conditions.

Note: The method of accounting predominantly used today is still based on historical cost accounting. There were many who supported historical cost accounting.

  • From 1950s, the levels of criticism increased, with many scholars prescribing different models providing more useful information than available under conventional historical cost.
  • In recent years, various accounting standards require or permit fair values when measuring assets (e.g., financial instruments, investment properties, some intangibles).
  • Historical cost accounting assumes that money holds a "constant purchasing power".
As Elliot states: "An implicit and troublesome assumption in the historical cost model is that the monetary unit is fixed and constant over time."

First Flaw: Historical cost accounting information suffers from problems of irrelevance in times of changing prices as it tends to "overstate profits" in times of rising prices.

أهلاً بكم يا دكاترة في الشابتر الخامس. هنا المحاضرة بتدخل في عمق النظريات المعيارية (Normative) اللي جاية تفرض بدائل وتعديلات لأن نظام المحاسبة التقليدي اللي شغالين بيه وهو التكلفة التاريخية (Historical Cost) فيه عيوب قاتلة وقت التضخم وغلاء الأسعار (Rising prices).

  • صدمة الواقع: المحاضرة بتقولك خد بالك! رغم عيوب التكلفة التاريخية، هي لسه النموذج السائد والمستخدم بكثرة في عالمنا النهاردة وفي ناس دافعوا عنها. لكن من الخمسينات والعلماء بيصرخوا ببدائل اعتمدتها الهيئات مؤخراً زي استخدام القيمة العادلة (Fair Value) في الأصول المالية والعقارات الاستثمارية.
  • فخ ثبات قيمة العملة: نظام التكلفة التاريخية قايم على فرضية خيالية إن الفلوس قوتها الشرائية ثابتة ومبتتغيرش!
📢 مقولة إليوت (Elliot) الشهيرة: بيقولك إن في افتراض "ضمني ومزعج" جوه التكلفة التاريخية إن الوحدة النقدية ثابتة ومستقرة عبر الزمن (fixed and constant)، وده طبعاً تضليل وقت الغلاء.

وعشان كده بيظهر العيب الأول الأساسي: المعلومات بتفقد الملاءمة بتاعتها (Irrelevance) وقت تغير الأسعار، لأن النظام ده بيضخم الأرباح بشكل وهمي (Overstate profits).

Distribution to shareholders of "historical cost profits" can actually lead to "erosion of operating capacity".

Numerical Example (Case 1):
• Company commenced operations with $100,000 inventory (20,000 units @ $5.00 each).
• At the end of the year, all inventory is sold for cash of $140,000.
• Profits under Historical Cost = $40,000 (This keeps financial capital intact, not operating capacity).
• If the entire $40,000 profit is distributed as dividends, financial capital remains $100,000.
The Reality Check (Case 2):
• To buy another 20,000 units to stay in business, the financial year-end replacement cost is now $5.40 per unit.
• Total funds required = 20,000 × $5.40 = $108,000.
• With the remaining $100,000, the firm can only buy 18,518 units (Operating capacity decreases!).

Correct Intact Measurement: Profit should accurately be recorded as:
$140,000 Revenue - (20,000 units × $5.40 Replacement Cost) = $32,000 operating profit.
If only $32,000 is distributed, the firm retains $108,000, keeping its ability to buy 20,000 units intact.

تعال نفهم الكارثة اللي التكلفة التاريخية بتعملها في السوق بمثال الأرقام اللي في المنهج، والمسألة دي بتوضح فكرة تآكل رأس المال المادي أو القدرة التشغيلية (Erosion of operating capacity):

🏁 بداية السنة: دخلنا بـ 100 ألف دولار عبارة عن بضاعة (20 ألف حتة، سعر الحتة 5 دولار).

بما إن الشركة معاها 100 ألف بس بعد توزيع الأرباح الوهمية، مش هتعرف تشتري غير 18,518 حتة بس! كده حجم الشركة كش وقدرتها التشغيلية تآكلت بسبب توزيع أرباح مش حقيقية.

Second Flaw: Historical cost distorts the current year's operating results by including holding gains that actually occurred in previous periods.

  • Example: Land acquired in 2004 for $1M. Fair value became $1.3M in 2008, $1.7M in 2011, and sold in 2012 for $1.9M.
  • Under HC, the entire profit of $900,000 is shown in 2012 alone, distorting that specific period and the preceding 8 years.

Third Flaw (Capital Maintenance Definitions): Hicks (1946) defined income as: "The maximum amount that can be consumed during the period, while still expecting to be as well off at the end of the period as at the beginning."

Perspective Measurement Core & Example Rule
Financial Capital Intact Profits earned only if ending money capital > beginning money capital ($140k - $100k = $40,000 profit). Taken in HC.
Purchasing Power Intact Adjusted via Price Index. Example: Land cost $1M multiplied by ending index 118 divided by opening index 100 = Adjusted cost $1,180,000. Adjusted Profit = $20,000 (instead of $200,000). Taken in CPPA.
Physical Operating Intact Profits earned if ending operating capacity > beginning capacity ($140k - [20k × $5.4] = $32,000 profit). Taken in CCA.

Fourth Flaw: Distortion of Return on Assets (ROA) measure.

  • Company A (Old) bought machinery for $1M, profit $100k ➡️ ROA = 10%.
  • Company B (New) bought identical machinery later for $2M due to inflation, profit $150k ➡️ ROA = 7.5% (Instead of 15% if evaluated at base cost).
  • Company B appears less efficient on paper purely due to historical cost accounting distortion!

One alternative to historical cost was CPPA, also called "general purchasing power accounting", "general price level accounting", or "constant dollar accounting".

  • It is based on the view that in times of rising prices, if an entity were to distribute unadjusted profits based on historical costs, it could be distributing part of its capital, reducing the real value of the entity.
  • The majority of early inflation literature relates to "restating historical financial costs" (CPP) as supplementary disclosures.
  • CPP accounting, with its reliance on "indices", is easier and less costly to apply than current valuation methods like CCA.

  • Broad Index: Based on a broad cross-section of consumed goods (General Price Index, such as Consumer Price Index CPI or Retail Price Index RPI).

When applying CPP, all adjustments are done at the end of the period and applied to accounts prepared under historical cost. Items must be strictly split into Monetary and Non-Monetary:

  • Monetary Assets: Remain fixed in terms of their monetary value (e.g., holding $10 cash). The monetary value is constant, but purchasing power decreases over time during inflation.
  • Non-Monetary Assets: Monetary value will change/increase over time as a result of inflation (e.g., property, plant, equipment, inventory). Their purchasing power remains constant over time.
  • Monetary Liabilities: Most liabilities are fixed in monetary terms because they represent an obligation to pay a specified amount of cash in the future, independent of currency purchasing power changes.
  • Non-Monetary Liabilities: Less common; includes obligations to transfer specific goods and services in the future, which can change in monetary value.
Net Monetary Assets = Monetary Assets - Monetary Liabilities
• Holders of monetary assets will lose because assets buy less at the end of the period.
• Conversely, holders of monetary liabilities will gain because they repay debts with money that is worth less in terms of real purchasing power.

Current Cost Accounting (CCA) is another alternative to historical cost accounting that tended to gain the most acceptance. It is based on "actual valuations" not adjusted historical cost.

  • Unlike historical cost, CCA strictly differentiates between profits from "trading" (operating) and "holding gains".
  • Holding gains can be realised or unrealised.
  • CCA (as proposed by Edward and Bell) adopts a physical capital maintenance approach, while financial capital intact is taken in historical cost accounting.
Core Definitions:
1. Valuations: Based on replacement cost; the amount that the entity would have to pay to replace an asset at the present time.
2. Operating Income: Represents realized revenues less the replacement cost of the assets.
3. Dividend Rule: Holding gains are excluded from operating profits and are not made available for dividends.

Example 1 (Land): Land initially acquired for $1,000,000 on Jan 1. At Dec 31, replacement cost increased by 18% (Adjusted Cost = $1,180,000), and it was sold for $1,200,000.

  • Historical Cost Profit = $1,200,000 - $1,000,000 = $200,000.
  • CCA Adjusted Profit (Trading) = $1,200,000 - $1,180,000 = $20,000.
  • Holding Gain = 1,180,000 - 1,000,000 = $180,000.

Example 2 (Inventory): Acquired 150 items @ $10 each. Sold 100 items for $15 each when replacement cost was $12. Year-end replacement cost for remaining 50 items was $14.

  • Historical Cost Profit = 100 × ($15 - $10) = $500.
  • CCA Operating Profit = 100 × ($15 - $12) = $300 (Available for dividends).
  • Realised Holding Gain = 100 × ($12 - $10) = $200 (Not available for dividends).
  • Unrealised Holding Gain = 50 × ($14 - $10) = $200 (Not available for dividends).

بص يا سيدي، طريقة المحاسبة بالتكلفة الجارية (CCA) دي البديل التاني والأكثر قبولاً للتكلفة التاريخية. العبقرية هنا إنها مش بتعدل الأرقام القديمة بمؤشر وخلاص زي الـ CPP، لا دي بتنزل السوق وتعتمد على التقييمات الفعلية (Actual valuations) للأصول بسعر النهاردة.

  • السر الأساسي للـ CCA: بتفصل تماماً بين نوعين من الأرباح؛ شطارتك في التجارة والبيع والشراء (Trading/Operating profit)، وبين مكاسب إن أصولك غليت في السوق لوحدها وأنت نايم (Holding gains) سواء المكاسب دي محققة بالبيع أو غير محققة والأصل لسه عندك.
  • مبدأ الحفاظ على الشركة: العلماء إدوارد وبيل (Edward & Bell) هم اللي حطوا الطريقة دي، وقالوا إنها قائمة على الحفاظ على رأس المال المادي أو الطاقة التشغيلية (Physical capital maintenance). وعشان كده بيحسبوا الأصول بـ تكلفة الإحلال (Replacement cost)، يعني الفلوس اللي تدفعها عشان تشتري أصل زي اللي عندك بالظبط بسعر النهاردة.
💡 تفكيك مثال المخزون (بيجي بالنص في المسائل):
شاريين 150 حتة بـ 10$. بعنا 100 حتة بـ 15$. وقت البيع سعرها في السوق بقى 12$. وباقي في المخزن 50 حتة سعرهم بقى 14$.
• ربح التشغيل الحقيقي: السعر اللي بعنا بيه ناقص سعرها في السوق وقت البيع $100 \times (15 - 12) = 300$. ده الربح الوحيد اللي ينفع يتوزع أرباح على المساهمين.
• مكسب حيازة محقق: البضاعة المباعة زادت في السوق من 10$ لـ 12$ وأنت محتفظ بيها ⬅️ $100 \times (12 - 10) = 200$.
• مكسب حيازة غير محقق: البضاعة اللي لسه في المخزن زادت من 10$ لـ 14$ ⬅️ $50 \times (14 - 10) = 200$.
⚠️ قاعدة بابل شيت: مكاسب الحيازة (Holding Gains) مستبعدة تماماً من أرباح التشغيل ومش مسموح نوزعها كـ Dividends عشان نحافظ على طاقة الشركة ونعرف نشتري بضاعة جديدة.

For non-current assets, under CCA, depreciation must be based on the replacement cost of the asset to determine current cost operating profits.

Depreciation Adjustment Example:
• Machine acquired at year-start for $100,000. Useful life = 10 years (Straight-line).
• Conventional Historical Cost Depreciation = $100,000 / 10 = $10,000 per year.
• At year-end, machine replacement cost increased to $120,000.
• CCA Depreciation Expense = $120,000 / 10 = $12,000 per year.
• A further $2,000 is deducted from operating profit (treated as a realized cost saving). The remaining $18,000 is an unrealized cost saving included in business profit.

Note: Like CPPA, no restatement of "monetary assets" is required in CCA because they are already recorded in current dollars. All "non-monetary assets" must be adjusted to replacement costs.

Criticisms of Current Cost Accounting (CCA):

  • Reliance on Replacement Cost: Replacement cost may not be the same for all firms; some firms may not choose to replace the asset at all.
  • It does not reflect what the asset would be worth if the firm decided to sell it (Exit Value).
  • Often very difficult to determine exact replacement costs in shifting markets.
  • Allocating replacement cost via depreciation is still arbitrary, just like historical cost.
  • Chambers (1995) claimed products of CCA were irrelevant and misleading.

Advantages of CCA:

  • Differentiating operating profit from holding gains enhances the usefulness of information.
  • Holding gains are due to market-wide movements that are completely beyond management's control, so isolating them evaluates management efficiency fairly.

In accounting research, studies are fundamentally labelled as either positive or normative:

  • Positive Research/Theory: Seeks to predict and explain particular phenomena and existing accounting practices. It provides a perspective on why managers, when confronted with a choice between competing accounting methods, elect to adopt one over others.
  • Normative Research/Theory: Seeks to prescribe the particular practice that should be undertaken. It is generated by applying a specific norm, standard, or objective against reality, and may suggest radical departures from actual practices (e.g., demanding Present Value measurement instead of Historical Cost during inflation).
  • Watts and Zimmerman (1986): Developed Positive Accounting Theory (PAT), stating it "is concerned with explaining accounting practice... but it says nothing as to which method a firm should use".
  • Other examples of positive frameworks include legitimacy theory, institutional theory, and stakeholder theory.

PAT relies entirely on strict economics-based behavioral assumptions:

  • The theory assumes that markets are efficient and all individuals' actions are driven completely by self-interest (the central assumption).
  • Individuals will act in an opportunistic manner to the extent that their actions will increase their personal wealth.
  • Notions of loyalty or morality are not incorporated in the theory.
  • Alignment Prediction: PAT predicts that firms will seek to put in place mechanisms that align the interests of the managers of the firm (the agents) with the interests of the owners of the firm (the principals).
  • Some of these alignment methods are tied directly to the outputs of the accounting system, such as bonus schemes providing managers with a share of the organization's profits.

الآليات دي بتعتمد على مخرجات النظام المحاسبي؛ يعني يربطوا مصلحة المدير بإنهم يدوله نسبة ومكافأة من "الأرباح المحاسبية السنوية الصافية". فبكده لما المدير يتشطر ويكبر أرباح الشركة عشان مصلحته، مصلحة المالك برضه بتتحقق والأسهم بتزيد.

PAT historically developed out of economics literature, heavily relying on three specific pillars:

  1. The Efficient Markets Hypothesis (EMH) regarding market data processing.
  2. Research on capital market reactions to accounting data, using models like the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM).
  3. The role of contracts in minimizing organizational conflicts, derived from Agency Theory.
Pillar 1: The Role of Efficient Markets Hypothesis (EMH)
• The EMH assumes that "capital markets react in an efficient and unbiased manner to publicly available information". Security prices reflect all information, not just accounting numbers.
• Since share prices accurately reflect true value, management cannot manipulate share prices by simply making cosmetic changes to accounting methods in an opportunistic manner.
• If a change in accounting methods does not signal a change in cash flow, EMH argues the capital market will not react. Security price changes only occur when beliefs about the present value of future cash flows alter.
• The Flaw of EMH alone: It provided evidence that markets react to cash-flow information, but it provided little explanation as to *why* managers choose specific accounting methods in the first place.
Pillar 2: Share Price Reactions to Unexpected Earnings
• Ball and Brown (1968) and Beaver (1968) investigated stock market reactions to earnings announcements.
• Ball and Brown (1968) proved that unexpected (new) information in accounting earnings leads to abnormal returns on corporate securities.
• To isolate these "abnormal returns", researchers used the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) to calculate what the "normal" expected return would have been based on the risk-free rate of return (government bonds) plus a risk/return component (stock volatility relative to the overall market).
• Abnormal Return = Actual Return - Expected (Normal) Return.

The Missing Link: While capital market literature throughout the 1970s proved a strong relationship between earnings and stock returns, it completely lacked hypotheses to predict and explain initial accounting choices. The ultimate key to explaining manager choices came from Agency Theory contracts.

A key to explaining managers' choice of particular accounting methods came from agency theory, which focuses on relationships between shareholders (principals) and managers (agents).

  • Jensen and Meckling (1976): Defined the agency relationship as a contract under which principals engage an agent to perform service on their behalf, delegating decision-making authority.
  • Principals assume the agent is driven entirely by self-interest and will undertake self-serving activities detrimental to the owners.
  • Managers have incentives to consume perquisites (privileges like luxury cars, medical coverage) and use confidential data for personal gain.
  • Price Protection: In the absence of restrictive contracts, owners anticipate opportunism and pay managers a lower salary to compensate for adverse actions.
  • Since managers prefer higher salaries, they have incentives to enter into contracts (tied to accounting numbers) to restrict their own opportunistic behavior.
The Three Agency Costs:
• Bonding costs: Costs involved in bonding agents' behaviour to expectations (e.g., preparing financial statements).
• Monitoring costs: Costs of monitoring agents' behaviour (e.g., auditing financial statements).
• Residual loss: Costs arising because it is too costly to remove all opportunistic behaviour.

بص يا سيدي، مفتاح السر اللي فهمنا ليه المديرين بيفضلوا طرق محاسبية معينة طلع من نظرية الوكالة (Agency Theory). النظرية دي بتبص للعلاقة بين الملاك والمساهمين أصحاب الشركة (Principals) وبين المديرين اللي بيحركوا الشغل (Agents).

  • دراسة جينسن وميكلينج (1976): دي الدراسة الأساسية اللي اعتمد عليها واتس وزيمرمان. عرفت العلاقة دي بإنها عقد بيفوض فيه المالك سلطة اتخاذ القرار للمدير. وبما إن المالك بيفترض إن المدير أناني وبيدور على مصلحته الشخصية بس، فالملاك بيبقوا متوقعين إن المدير هيبعزق فلوس الشركة على مصلحته ورفاهيته الشخصية ويستهلك امتيازات فخمة (Perquisites) زي العربيات الفارهة والعلاج السياحي على حساب الشركة!
  • آلية حماية السعر (Price Protection): لو مفيش عقود تقيد المدير، المالك بيحمي نفسه ذكياً؛ بيدي للمدير مرتب قليل عشان يعوض الخساير والعبث اللي المدير هيعمله! وبما إن المدير طبعاً عايز مرتب كبير، بيبقى عنده دافع إنه يوافق يدخل في عقود واتفاقيات (Contracts) تقيد حركته وتثبت أمانته للملاك، والعقود دي بتبقى مربوطة بأرقام المحاسبة.
💰 تكاليف الوكالة (Agency Costs) بتنقسم في الامتحان لـ ٣ أنواع:
١. تكاليف الارتباط (Bonding costs): دي الفلوس اللي الشركة بتصرفها عشان تثبت إن المدير ماشي صح (زي تكلفة إعداد القوائم المالية بانتظام).
٢. تكاليف الرقابة (Monitoring costs): دي الفلوس اللي الملاك بيصرفوها عشان يراقبوا المدير (وأشهر مثال ليها بالنص هو: تكلفة مراجعة الحسابات والـ Auditing).
٣. الخسارة المتبقية (Residual loss): الخسارة اللي هتحصل هتحصل، لأن مستحيل عملياً وتكلفةً نمنع سلوك المدير الانتهازي بنسبة 100%.

In 1978, Watts and Zimmerman introduced three key hypotheses used to explain and predict whether managers would support or oppose a specific accounting method:

🎯 1. Bonus Plan Hypothesis (Management Compensation Hypothesis):
Predicts that managers of firms with bonus plans tied to reported income are more likely to use accounting methods that increase current year reported income (e.g., using FIFO during inflation or longer depreciation periods) to maximize the present value of their bonuses.
🎯 2. Debt Hypothesis (Debt/Equity Hypothesis):
Predicts that the higher the firm's debt/equity ratio, the more likely managers are to use accounting methods that increase income. High ratio means the firm is close to constraints in debt covenants (restrictions in loan contracts).
Closer constraints increase the probability of technical default (violating terms of the loan without missing payments), which triggers negative events like interest rate hikes. Managers thus choose methods that relax these constraints.
🎯 3. Political Cost Hypothesis:
Predicts that large firms rather than small firms are more likely to use accounting choices that reduce reported profits (e.g., using LIFO during inflation or accelerated depreciation). Size acts as a proxy for political attention. Managers reduce income to minimize scrutiny from government and lobby groups.

Research that applies PAT typically adopts one of two distinct viewpoints regarding managerial behavior:

  • Opportunistic Perspective: Assumes managers choose accounting methods to serve their own self-interest and maximize personal wealth at the expense of owners. Watts and Zimmerman's early work was grounded here.
  • Efficiency Perspective: Proposes that managers elect a particular accounting method because it most efficiently reflects the underlying firm performance and minimizes contracting costs.
  • Example: Under the efficiency lens, a manager selects a specific depreciation method not to increase his bonus (Opportunistic), but because it accurately mirrors the actual economic wear and tear of the asset.

Firms utilize two primary perspectives within PAT to analyze accounting choices:

1. Efficiency Perspective (also known as Ex Ante perspective):
• Considers what mechanisms are put in place up-front (before contractual arrangements) with the objective of minimizing future agency and contracting costs.
• Agency costs: Costs inherent in the principal-agent relationship.
• Contracting costs: The tracking of costs associated with a specific contract. These include: search and information costs, bargaining and decision costs, drafting, policing, and enforcement costs.
• Example: Many firms voluntarily prepared financial statements and subjected them to an audit before any regulatory requirement existed. Audits provide external parties with more reliable data, enabling the firm to attract funds at a lower cost, reducing investor risk, decreasing the cost of capital, and thereby increasing firm value.
• Under this lens, firms adopt methods that best reflect the underlying financial performance, minimizing the need for investors to gather additional external data, leading to vast cost savings. Regulation forcing specific methods imposes unwarranted costs.
2. Opportunistic Perspective (also known as Ex Post perspective):
• Considers the opportunistic actions that could be undertaken once various contractual arrangements have been put in place.
• While arrangements are initially assumed to be put in place to reduce agency costs and align interests (Efficiency), once they are set, managers will adopt manipulative strategies to generate the greatest economic benefits to themselves.
• Example: Once a bonus scheme based on profits is negotiated, managers could elect accounting methods that artificially increase income (e.g., choosing an asset depreciation method over a longer useful life) to boost their bonus size, even if it does not reflect the actual economic use of the asset.

Critical Limitation Note: In reality, it is highly difficult to determine whether opportunistic or efficiency considerations drove a manager's choice, which remains a primary limitation of much PAT research.

أبحاث نظرية المحاسبة الإيجابية (PAT) بتدرس السلوك من خلال نظارتين وعلينا فهمهم بعمق:

  • ١. منظور الكفاءة (Efficiency / Ex Ante): كلمة Ex Ante يعني "مقدماً أو من قبل ما نمضي العقد". هنا الملاك بيفكروا إزاي يحطوا نظام ونقاط مراقبة من الأول خالص عشان يقللوا تكاليف الوكالة وتكاليف التعاقد المستقبلية (Contracting costs) زي تكاليف البحث، التفاوض، الصياغة، والفرض والالتزام.
    • مثال ذكي جداً: زمان الشركات كانت بتنشر قوائم مالية وبتجيب مراجع حسابات خارجي يراجعها اختيارياً وطوعياً من قبل ما تظهر القوانين وتفرض ده! طب ليه بيغرموا نفسهم؟ عشان المراجعة بتدي مصداقية بره، فالشركة تقدر تجيب قروض وتمويل بفوائد رخيصة (Lower cost of capital)، وتقلل المخاطر على المستثمرين وتكبر قيمة الشركة! فالاختيار هنا قايم على الكفاءة وأنه بيعبر عن الأداء بأدق صورة وتوفير تكاليف جمع المعلومات على المستثمر.
  • ٢. المنظور الانتهازي (Opportunistic / Ex Post): كلمة Ex Post يعني "لاحقاً أو بعد ما الفأس تقع في الرأس ونمضي العقد". إحنا بنعمل العقود والمكافآت في الأول بنية صافية عشان نحقق الكفاءة (Efficiency)، بس أول ما العقود دي بتمضي وتتثبت، المدير بيقلب وبيركب المنظور الانتهازي! بيبدأ يدور على أي ثغرة أو تلاعب (Manipulative strategies) يعظم بيها مصلحته الشخصية.
    • مثال: عملنا له عقد بياخد حوافز مربوطة بالربح، أول ما العقد يشتغل يروح منقي طريقة محاسبية تضخم أرباح السنة دي (زي إنه يحسب إهلاك أصول على فترة طويلة جداً مبالغ فيها) عشان يزود رقم ربحه وبالتالي يكبر حجم المكافأة اللي هتدخل جيبه، حتى لو الطريقة دي مش بتعكس الاستهلاك الحقيقي للأصل!
⚠️ عيب خطير في الأبحاث: المحاضرة بتقولك إن في الواقع العملي صعب جداً تفصل وتجزم هل المدير اختار الطريقة دي بدافع الكفاءة ولا بدافع الانتهازية، وده بيعتبر من أكبر العيوب والقيود (Limitations) اللي بتواجه أبحاث نظرية المحاسبة الإيجابية.

The core contractual arrangements address mechanics between owners and managers based on the accounting system outputs:

  • Central Baseline: PAT assumes manager actions are driven purely by self-interest, meaning owners (principals) expect managers (agents) to act against owner welfare if left unchecked.
  • Information Asymmetry: Because of their direct operational position, managers have deep access to internal information that is completely unavailable to principals.
  • This asymmetry enhances the manager's capability to execute self-serving actions at the explicit expense of the owners.
  • Price Protect Rule: In the total absence of structural controls, owners will proactively compensate for this expected behavior by paying managers a lower salary.
  • The Shift: Since managers naturally prefer higher salaries, this creates an embedded incentive for managers to willingly sign contractual arrangements that legally minimize their capacity to harm owner interests, thereby securing higher base compensation.

Firms design structural remuneration schemes using two main strategies to align motivations:

1. Fixed Basis: Salary is completely independent of performance.

  • Behavioral Result: Under self-interest, a manager on a purely fixed salary will avoid taking great business risks because he/she does not share in potential upside gains, creating limited incentive to maximize corporate value.

2. Salary Plus Remuneration (Bonus Schemes): Salary is tied directly to firm performance to align manager-owner goals.

  • In accounting-based designs, rewards are tied to: Profits of the firm, Sales of the firm, or Return on Assets (ROA).
  • Risks of Accounting Bonus Plans: Because payments tie directly to numbers, changes in methods (or new standards) shift bonus sizes. This induces managers to manipulate accounting numbers to improve apparent rewards (Opportunistic Perspective) and forces a dangerous short-term focus. Managers will avoid long-term projects with delayed or non-uniform periodic cash flows.

3. Market-Based Bonus Schemes: Remuneration tied to the market value of the firm's securities.

  • PAT theorists argue market schemes are superior when accounting earnings fluctuate wildly, such as in mining firms or high-technology R&D firms. Market value reflects long-term expectations of the present value of future cash flows.
  • Methods: Cash bonuses based on share price increases, or providing share options to buy stock, creating incentives to boost true firm value.
  • Associated Problem: Share prices are heavily influenced by macro factors and market-wide movements completely beyond the control of managers.

In contrast to PAT, Chapter 8 introduces alternative perspectives called systems-oriented theories or open-system theories. These include Legitimacy Theory, Stakeholder Theory, and Institutional Theory.

  • These frameworks provide "explanations and predictions" for managerial actions, such as the voluntary decision to disclose environmental/social data. Thus, they are deemed Positive Theories (not Normative).
  • Accounting disclosure policies are viewed as a "strategy" to influence the organization's relationships with external parties. They explain voluntary CSR reporting.
  • They focus entirely on the role of "information and disclosure" in relationships between organizations, the State, individuals, and groups. The entity is viewed as part of a wider social system: it influences and is heavily influenced by society.
Political Economy Theory (The Umbrella Framework):
• Defined as: "The social, political and economic framework within which human life takes place."
• Society, politics, and economics are completely inseparable. Financial issues cannot be properly investigated in isolation from this institutional framework.
• Corporate reports are not neutral or unbiased documents; they are a product of interchange between the firm and its environment, attempting to mediate and accommodate sectional interests.

🌍 المظلة الكبرى - نظرية الاقتصاد السياسي (Political Economy):
المحاضرة بتقولك إن النظريات الثلاثة مشتقين من نظرية أكبر وأوسع اسمها الاقتصاد السياسي، وهو الإطار الاجتماعي والسياسي والاقتصادي اللي بنعيش فيه.
فكرتها الأساسية إن المجتمع والسياسة والاقتصاد حاجة واحدة ومينفعش نفصلهم عن بعض، والتقارير المالية للشركات ليست وثائق محايدة أو غير متحيزة، دي منتج طالع من التفاعل بين الشركة وبيئتها عشان ترضي وتوفق بين مصالح الفئات المختلفة في المجتمع.

Legitimacy Theory asserts that organizations continually seek to ensure that they operate within the bounds and norms of their respective societies. They attempt to ensure their activities are perceived as "legitimate".

  • These bounds and norms are not fixed; they change over time, requiring the firm to be highly responsive to its environment.
  • Legitimacy VS Legitimation:
    • Legitimacy: A status or condition which exists when an entity's value system is congruent/agrees with the value system of society.
    • Legituation: The active process that leads to an organization being adjudged legitimate.
  • For legitimacy, actual conduct is not what matters; it is what society collectively knows or perceives about the conduct. A firm can diverge from norms but retain legitimacy if the divergence goes unnoticed. It is a social construct based on attitudes.
The Concept of The Social Contract:
• Used to represent the multitude of implicit (non-legislated) and explicit (legislated) expectations society has about how the firm should operate.
• Laws provide explicit terms, while non-legislated societal expectations provide implicit terms. Law and societal norms have an imperfect correlation because legal systems are slow to adapt to changing norms.
• Due to changing public expectations, firms must now address CSR (environmental, human, and safety issues). Failing to comply leads to heavy societal sanctions (legal restrictions, limited resources, or reduced product demand).

تعالوا ندخل في النظرية الأولى بالتفصيل وهي نظرية الشرعية (Legitimacy Theory):

  • الهدف الأساسي: النظرية بتقول إن الشركات طول الوقت بتسعى وتكافح عشان تبان في عيون المجتمع إن تصرفاتها شرعية (Legitimate)، وماشية جوه برواز العادات والقيم السائدة في المجتمع اللي هي شغالة فيه. والقيم دي مش ثابتة دي بتتغير من جيل لجيل.
  • الفرق بين المصطلحين (مهم جداً):
    • الشرعية (Legitimacy): دي "الحالة أو الوضع" لما تلاقي قيم وأهداف الشركة ماشية في نفس اتجاه قيم وأهداف المجتمع.
    • إضفاء الشرعية (Legituation): دي "العملية أو الخطوات السلوكية" اللي الشركة بتعملها عشان تخلي المجتمع يحكم عليها إنها شرعية ومحترمة.
  • العين هي اللي بتحكم: في نظرية الشرعية، مش مهم الشركة بتعمل إيه في الحقيقة، المهم المجتمع شايفها وشايف تصرفاتها إزاي (Perception)! يعني لو شركة بتلوث البيئة من ورا الناس ومحدش قفشها، هي لسه محتفظة بشرعيتها لأن انحرافها ملوش صدى والناس ملمحتوش! فالشرعية هي "بناء اجتماعي" (Social construct) بيقيس رضا المجتمع عن سلوكك.
📜 لغز العقد الاجتماعي (The Social Contract):
النظرية قايمة على فكرة إن في "عقد اجتماعي وهمي" بين الشركة والمجتمع. العقد ده له شروط:
• شروط صريحة (Explicit): دي القوانين والتشريعات اللي الدولة بتفرضها والمحاكم بتحاسب عليها.
• شروط ضمنية (Implicit): دي توقعات وأعراف المجتمع اللي مش مكتوبة في قانون (زي إن المجتمع يرفض سلوك معين مع إنه مش ممنوع بالقانون). وطبعاً القانون دايماً بطيء في ملاحقة أعراف وتغيرات المجتمع.
⚠️ عقوبات المجتمع (Sanctions): لو الشركة أخلت بالعقد الاجتماعي ده وبقت ريحتها وحشة ومبتهتمش بالبيئة والصحة (CSR)، المجتمع هيفرض عليها عقوبات فورية؛ زي مقاطعة منتجاتها، أو فرض قيود قانونية عليها، أو منع الموارد عنها فتقفل!

Stakeholder Theory focuses on specific groups within society (Stakeholder groups) rather than society as a whole. It accepts that different groups have different views, leading to multiple negotiated contracts.

  • Similarities with Legitimacy: Both are overlapping perspectives set within the 'political economy' framework, seeing the firm as part of a broader social system.
  • Stakeholder Definition: Any identifiable group or individual who can affect or is affected by the achievement of an organization's objectives. They split into:
    • Primary Stakeholders: Without whose continuing participation the corporation cannot survive as a going concern (e.g., shareholders, labor, finance).
    • Secondary Stakeholders: Those who influence or are influenced by the firm, but are not essential for its survival and not engaged in transactions.
1. The Ethical (Moral / Normative) Branch:
• Argues that all stakeholders have the right to be treated fairly by an organization. Issues of stakeholder power are completely irrelevant.
• Management must give equal consideration to the interests of all stakeholders, regardless of whether this leads to improved financial performance. Business should benefit everyone; if interests conflict, management must attain the optimal balance.
2. The Managerial (Positive) Branch:
• Attempts to explain when corporate management will be likely to attend to the expectations of particular (powerful) stakeholders.
• The firm will not respond to all stakeholders, but will respond to the most powerful groups. Stakeholder power is a function of their degree of control over crucial resources required by the organization (e.g., labor, finance, influential media, ability to legislate).
• Information Strategic Role: Financial accounting and social disclosures are a major element employed to manage stakeholders to gain support/approval or distract opposition/disapproval.

    • أصحاب مصلحة ثانويين (Secondary): بيأثروا ويتأثروا بس مش أساسيين لبقاء الشركة ومش داخلين في معاملات مباشرة معاها زي الحركات البيئية أو جماعات الضغط.

• مراعاة الكل بغض النظر هل ده هيزود أرباح الفلوس ولا لأ regardless of financial performance ⬅️ اختار برضه الفرع الأخلاقي / المعياري Ethical branch.
• فئة من غيرها الشركة تموت وتقفل وم تكملش cannot survive as a going concern ⬅️ دول الأوليين Primary stakeholders.
• فئة بتأثر وتتأثر بس مش أساسية للبقاء not essential for its survival ⬅️ دول الثانويين Secondary stakeholders.
• بيشرح امتى الإدارة بتهتم وتلبي طلبات فئات معينة قوية expectations of powerful stakeholders ⬅️ اختار الفرع الإداري البراجماتي Managerial branch of Stakeholder Theory.
